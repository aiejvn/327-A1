# Requirements Analysis: Trailside Outdoor Gear Rental

The brief has one stakeholder voice (the shop owner). Rows 1-8 are drawn from the owner's statement. Rows 9-14 are implied by it and need validation in the role-play. In the quality columns, ✅ = OK, ⚠️ = partial or at risk, ❌ = fails.

| # | Requirement (as written) | Source (role/stakeholders) | Conflict | Ambiguity | Completeness | Consistency | Testability | Notes/Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Tracks what gear we have" | Shop owner | ✅ None | ⚠️ "Gear" is undefined: individual items or types? Which attributes? | ❌ No fields (ID, category, size, condition, status, replacement value) | ✅ | ⚠️ Testable only once attributes and statuses are defined | Treat each of the ~150 pieces as a uniquely identified item with a status (available / rented / damaged / missing / retired). Confirm with counter staff and gear manager. |
| 2 | "Who's renting what" | Shop owner | ✅ | ⚠️ What identifies "who"? Is a name enough, or are phone, ID, and email needed? Can one renter take multiple items? | ❌ No customer data, group rentals, or check-out process defined | ✅ | ⚠️ Needs a defined check-out flow to test | Link each rental record to one customer and one or more specific items. Ask staff what they collect today, and ask what customers will tolerate at a busy counter. |
| 3 | "When it's due back" | Shop owner | ✅ | ⚠️ Due date only, or date and time? Hourly, half-day, daily, or weekend rentals? | ⚠️ No mention of overdue alerts, extensions, or late fees | ✅ | ⚠️ "Due" needs a defined time unit and an overdue rule | Define a due date/time per rental and a "due today / overdue" view. Ask about late-return policy and extensions. |
| 4 | "We're losing track of things during busy weekends" | Shop owner | ⚠️ Speed under load conflicts with capturing detailed data (see #8) | ❌ "Losing track" and "busy" are vague | ❌ No target for check-out/return speed or concurrency | ✅ | ❌ Not measurable | Restate as a measurable requirement, e.g. "Staff can check out a rental in under X seconds or steps during peak load." Get X from counter staff. |
| 5 | "Some gear has gone missing or come back damaged without us really knowing who had it last" | Shop owner | ⚠️ Accountability needs detail that "keep it simple" discourages | ⚠️ "Knowing who had it last" could mean a full history or just the last renter | ❌ No damage or loss workflow: who records it, when, what happens next (charge? repair?) | ✅ | ⚠️ Testable ("show last 3 renters of item X"), but the retention period is unspecified | Keep a per-item rental history and record condition at check-out and return. Clarify whether customers are billed for damage. |
| 6 | "About 150 pieces of gear" | Shop owner | ✅ | ⚠️ "About": is it fixed or growing? | ⚠️ No growth or seasonality info | ✅ | ✅ Verifiable as a data-volume target | Support at least 150 items now, with headroom (e.g. 300) for growth. Confirm with the owner. |
| 7 | "Maybe 50 rentals on a busy weekend" | Shop owner | ✅ | ⚠️ "Maybe": is 50 the peak or typical? Rentals per day or per weekend? How many staff use it at once? | ⚠️ No concurrent-user count or peak-hour distribution | ✅ | ✅ Can be load-tested once a number is fixed | Take 50 rentals per weekend as a baseline and confirm the peak with staff. Likely a single-location, low-concurrency system. |
| 8 | "Keep it simple" | Shop owner | ⚠️ Conflicts with #4 and #5 (more accountability means more data entry) and with the implied requirements #9-#14 | ❌ Highly subjective: simple for whom (staff, owner, customers)? | ❌ No definition of in or out of scope | ⚠️ Could contradict later requests | ❌ Not testable as written | Convert to concrete constraints, e.g. "a new staff member can complete a check-out after 5 minutes of training" or "at most N fields per transaction". Use it as a scope guard for prioritization. |
| 9 | *(Implied)* Replace the paper logbook and whiteboard | Inferred from owner; validate with staff | ✅ | ⚠️ Replace entirely or run in parallel? | ❌ No plan to migrate existing paper records | ✅ | ⚠️ "Replaces" is testable only with defined cut-over criteria | Ask what the whiteboard gives staff at a glance (probably who has what and what's due today). The system needs an equivalent at-a-glance view. |
| 10 | *(Implied)* Check availability before renting | Inferred; validate with counter staff | ✅ | ⚠️ Live availability only, or advance reservations? | ❌ Reservations, walk-ins, and holds are not mentioned | ✅ | ✅ (e.g. an "available items by type" list) | Ask whether the shop takes phone or online reservations. If not, keep it to real-time availability. |
| 11 | *(Implied)* Record gear return and condition | Inferred; validate with staff and gear manager | ✅ | ⚠️ Define "damaged": scale of damage, photos, notes? | ❌ Cleaning, repair, and out-of-service statuses are missing | ✅ | ✅ (return, then status and notes are updated) | Add a return step with a condition check and an "out of service" status that blocks re-renting. |
| 12 | *(Implied)* Payment, pricing, and deposits | Owner (probably); validate | ⚠️ May conflict with "keep it simple" | ❌ Never mentioned: is billing in scope? | ❌ Rates, deposits, damage fees, and discounts are all unknown | ✅ | ❌ Nothing to test yet | Ask the owner whether the system tracks money or only gear, and what is charged for damaged or lost items. |
| 13 | *(Implied)* Roles and access (staff vs owner) | Inferred; owner and staff | ✅ | ⚠️ Unknown | ❌ Who can edit records, retire gear, or waive fees? | ✅ | ✅ Once roles are defined | Ask whether the owner needs reports or an override, or whether all staff have the same rights. |
| 14 | *(Implied)* Reporting and history (missing, damaged, overdue, utilization) | Owner | ✅ | ⚠️ Which reports? | ❌ No report list or frequency | ✅ | ✅ Once defined | Ask the owner what decisions they want to make, e.g. which gear to replace or which customers to flag. |
